详细描述

Persistent CAD material appearance exposed by the Viewer SDK.

Transparency uses the CAD-facing convention: 0.0 means opaque and 1.0 means fully transparent. The alpha component of color is retained for convenient DTO transport; transparency is the authoritative alpha value for this API.
